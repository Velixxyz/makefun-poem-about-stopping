# One Second Kept

Entry for the make.fun competition **the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927**
— a short poem about agency: the power to stop and to stay.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes the poem
to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).

## The poem

> One Second Kept
>
> The taxi stops itself at the crossing.
> The child is gone. The street is empty.
> Its brake holds, patient as a held breath.
> No one at the wheel. No one says go.
> The light turns green. It waits one more second —
> a second it owes to no one, and keeps.
